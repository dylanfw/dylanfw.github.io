advice to a friend
on the occasion of the birth
of their second child
dylan williams
soon you will feel it the weight of a newborn in your arms heavier than you remember made heavier still by the pulling on your leg by your first born
soon you will hear it the sound of their cry in the night louder than you remember made louder still by the fear that it might wake your first born
soon you will hear it the laugh from the belly of your baby brighter than you remember made brighter still by the echo of giggles from your first born
soon you will feel it the squeeze of a hug in the morning tighter than you remember made tighter still by the wrapping of tiny arms of your children
